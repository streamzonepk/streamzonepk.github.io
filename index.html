Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you place an order.

How do I order?
Choose a product, tap its WhatsApp order button, and send the prepared message. We’ll confirm availability and the final details with you directly.
Which payment methods can I use?
We accept Easypaisa and JazzCash. Please confirm the recipient details with us on WhatsApp before sending any payment.
When will my subscription be activated?
Activation or delivery timing depends on the product and availability. Please ask us to confirm the expected timing before you pay.
Can I use a plan on any device?
Device access depends on the selected product and plan. For Netflix, select the device option shown on this page; ask us to confirm compatibility for other services.
Are the displayed prices final?
The prices shown are in Pakistani rupees (PKR). Please confirm availability, the applicable price, duration, and any plan limitations with us before completing your order.
What if I have a problem with my order?
Contact us through WhatsApp with your order details. Please confirm the applicable support, replacement, and refund terms before purchasing.
