Privacy notice

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This notice explains what information Stream Zone collects when you use this website and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, WhatsApp number and password when you create an account. Passwords are handled by our account provider and we can't see them.
  • Order details: the products you order, prices, payment method and any note you add.
  • Payment receipts: the screenshot you upload so we can confirm your payment.
  • Complaints: the messages and screenshots you send us about an order.
  • Browser storage: your cart and saved products are kept in your own browser.

2. How we use it

We use your information to process and deliver your orders, check payments, send your login details, reply to complaints and contact you about your order by email or WhatsApp. We don't sell your information.

3. Where it's stored

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Accounts, orders, receipts and complaints are stored with Supabase, our database provider, and only you and Stream Zone staff can see your orders and receipts. Order emails are sent through an email delivery provider.

4. WhatsApp

If you message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp's own privacy terms apply to that conversation.

5. Your choices

You can update your name and WhatsApp number from your account's Profile page. To delete your account or ask what information we hold about you, message us on WhatsApp at +92 309 4839648.

6. Keep your details safe

Never put passwords, PINs, card numbers or verification codes in order notes or complaints.
