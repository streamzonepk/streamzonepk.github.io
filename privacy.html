Privacy notice

Last updated: 17 October 2026

We want you to understand what information may be shared when you browse Stream Zone or contact us about an order.

1. Information collected on this website

This website does not provide a checkout form or ask you to create an account. When you tap an order or contact link, you choose whether to open WhatsApp and send a message. The website may be hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical information such as standard server logs as part of operating the hosting service.

2. Messages and order information

If you contact us on WhatsApp, the information you choose to send—such as your name, selected product, and order questions—may be used to reply, confirm availability, coordinate payment, and provide order support. WhatsApp is a separate service and its own privacy terms apply to your use of it.

3. Payment safety

Confirm the recipient name and payment instructions with us before sending money. Never send your payment PIN, password, or one-time verification code. Share only the information needed to handle your enquiry or order.

4. Cookies, analytics, and third parties

We do not intentionally use this website to collect payment-card details or run a customer account system. If analytics, advertising tools, embedded services, or other data collection features are added in the future, this notice should be updated to explain them.

5. Data requests and contact

For a question about information you sent us through WhatsApp, contact us and identify the relevant conversation or order. We may need to retain some order messages for customer support or recordkeeping, where appropriate.
