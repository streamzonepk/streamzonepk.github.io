Ordering terms

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Please read these points before placing an order with Stream Zone.

1. Enquiries and order confirmation

Orders are handled manually through WhatsApp; submitting an enquiry is not confirmation that a product is available. Before paying, ask us to confirm the exact product, subscription duration, device or account arrangement, total price, expected activation time, and any limitations that apply.

2. Prices and availability

Website prices are shown in Pakistani rupees (PKR). Availability, fulfilment details, and the applicable price must be confirmed with us before payment. Do not pay based only on an unconfirmed message or an assumption about what a plan includes.

3. Payment safety

We currently list Easypaisa and JazzCash as payment methods. Confirm the recipient name and instructions directly with us before sending payment. Keep your transaction receipt. We will never need your payment PIN, account password, or one-time verification code.

4. Activation, support, and refunds

Activation timing depends on product availability and must be confirmed before payment. Ask us to explain the applicable support, replacement, cancellation, and refund terms for your specific order before you pay. Do not assume a refund or replacement is guaranteed unless the terms have been explicitly confirmed.

5. Provider terms and lawful use

Customers are responsible for using services in line with the relevant provider's terms. Stream Zone should offer only subscriptions it is authorized to supply and IPTV content that is legally licensed. Brand names and logos belong to their respective owners; Stream Zone is an independent storefront and does not claim official affiliation with those providers.

6. Reviews and public submissions

Reviews submitted through the website are displayed publicly and may be read by any visitor. Do not include confidential or sensitive information. We may remove reviews that contain personal information, spam, abusive content, or material unrelated to a genuine customer experience. Contact us on WhatsApp if you want to request a correction or removal.

7. Questions or order issues

Contact us on WhatsApp and include the product name and any order reference you have. Do not send sensitive login, payment, or identity information unless it is genuinely necessary and you understand why it is needed.

Contact Stream Zone on WhatsApp
